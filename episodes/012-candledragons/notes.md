# 012 — Candle Dragons
Angle: escalation
Mode: wild (silly, castle at night) — opener: cold-open (pick_mode). Hook = line 32 (the giant fire dragon lowering its glowing head toward Wren on the castle wall).
Premise: Blow out a candle and its flame hatches into a tiny fire dragon (flames don't go out — they fly off). Close your eyes and wish first, and the dragon flies back into the wick.
Logline: On her eleventh birthday, Wren blows out all eleven candles without making a wish — her eleven tiny dragons eat every torch in the castle and merge into one giant dragon on the wall, until she remembers how Grandma does it, closes her eyes and wishes them a home.
Twist (planted): Grandma "always closes her eyes before she blows" and her dragons fly back into the wick (10-11); Wren blows in one excited breath (14). Payoff: "Did you make your wish?" (28) — Wren closes her eyes like Grandma (33) and wishes the dragons a home; they burst into sparks and relight every torch (35-37).
Characters:
- Wren, 10→11 (narrator, the Queen's granddaughter): tan skin, dark-brown pigtails, freckles, emerald tunic, short red cape, silver circlet with a green gem, belt, brown boots.
- Grandma, the Queen: light skin, white bun, wrinkles, gold crown, royal-purple robe + cape, white sash.
- Baker Hob: brown skin, black walrus mustache, tall chef hat (new outfit piece), cream shirt + white apron.
- Sir Bram: yellow skin, full ginger beard, open-face knight helm with a red plume (new outfit piece), armour over a blue surcoat; a tiny dragon sleeps on his helmet.
- Guards (no lines): knight helms with yellow / blue plumes, red and green surcoats.
