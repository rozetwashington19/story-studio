# 024 — My Cat Got My Promotion
Mode: wild (silly, all ages: office humour, a goth vet, a CEO who fires people for blinking; funny twist ending with a small warm beat) — opener: cold-open (pick_mode). Hook = line 58 (Nina's body behind the VICE PRESIDENT desk, sweeping the #1 BOSS mug off it while Mrs. Stone applauds).
Angle: role-reversal — the narrator swaps places with her cat, learns the cat's side (nineteen hours of sleep, no emails, no rent) while the cat learns hers (meetings, spreadsheets, "synergy") — and then refuses to swap back.
Premise: Every time my cat sneezes, we swap bodies.
Logline: Every time Nina's cat Beans sneezes they swap bodies — so when Beans sneezes inside the bag at the biggest job interview of Nina's life, a cat in Nina's body stares down the scariest CEO in the city, pushes her coffee off the desk, naps in the sunbeam, chases a laser pointer across the boardroom and gets hired as vice president; the "allergy" turns out to be a pepper shaker Beans has been sniffing on purpose, and when a week of meetings sends her crawling home to beg for her old life back, Nina (the cat) pushes the pepper off the fridge. Slowly.

I make biscuits."
