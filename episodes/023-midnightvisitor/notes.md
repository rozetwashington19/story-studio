# 023 — The Midnight Visitor
Mode: wild (silly-spooky, all ages: a creepy "thing" that lands every night turns out to be a lost dad in a spacesuit; the last lines flip who the aliens are) — opener: cold-open (pick_mode). Hook = line 23 (the gold visor pressed against Opal's window, tapping on the glass).
Angle: trick-ending — the last lines flip the whole premise: the helmet comes off ("two eyes, one nose, no antennae at all"), Dad whispers "Aliens. I just knew it", every hat lifts on a pair of antennae, and the final pull-back shows the back yard is on Mars (WELCOME TO MARS, pop. 412 and one dog).
Premise: Every night at midnight, something lands behind our house. Nobody knows what it wants; it walks slow, breathes loud, and has one giant golden eye.
Logline: Every midnight a slow, loud-breathing thing with one giant golden eye lands behind fourteen-year-old Opal's house and carries off the garden gnome, the neighbour's dirt, the barbecue and the mailbox — so she follows it to a dented little ship full of neatly labelled "specimens" and a kid's crayon drawing, wakes the whole street to fix its engine, and when it finally takes off its head the family's antennae pop up: the monster is a lost dad from a tiny blue dot called Earth, and we were on Mars the whole time.
Twist / surprising beats: it leaves a little flag where the gnome stood; SPECIMEN 4 "small statue of local king"; the drawing (COME HOME SOON) — "it wasn't a monster, it was somebody's dad"; the engine cough Opal recognises from Dad's lawn mower; Dad fixes a spaceship in four minutes (the mower: nine years); "I come in peace, take me to your leader" → "Aliens," Dad whispered; antennae through every hat (dog included); Earth: "never heard of it"; "Greetings from Mars."
Gags: Monday the gnome (a flag in its place); Tuesday Mrs. Bell's flower bed (it only took the dirt — callback: "we let it keep the dirt"); Wednesday it pokes the dog with a probe and writes FIELD NOTES ("4 legs, VERY angry, do not poke again"); Dad's traps (net over FREE ROCKS, pit under a WELCOME mat, a very angry sprinkler) catch the mailman, twice; the net catches the thing, which keeps bouncing — with the net, and with Dad.
Re-watch clues: everybody wears a hat, always (Opal's beanie, Dad's rancher hat, Finn's bike helmet, Mrs. Bell's head scarf, the mailman's cap); the butterscotch evening sky and rust-red dunes behind the fence; the visitor bounces in our low gravity; it photographs a mailbox and steals dirt.
