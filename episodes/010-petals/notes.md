# 010 — Petals
Mode: heartfelt — opener: in-this-world (pick_mode). Hook = line 23 (the petal river pouring into Grandpa's window).
Premise: In this world, when someone misses you, petals fall on you — out of nowhere, wherever you are.
Logline: Pink petals fall on eleven-year-old Ada every single day, even though everyone who loves her is right there — until she follows them on the wind to Sunny Pines, where Grandpa Walt, who forgets her name now, has never stopped missing her.
Twist (planted): she says "everyone who loves me is right here" (11); petals drift one way (19-20); she stopped visiting because he called her by Mom's name (25-27). Payoff: the petals stop when she sits with him, then one lands on him (36-38).
