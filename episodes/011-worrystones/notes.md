# 011 — Worry Stones
Mode: heartfelt — opener: cold-open (pick_mode). Hook = line 30 (June holding a worry stone with MILO written on it by the lighthouse lens).
Premise: Every worry you have goes into your backpack as a heavy stone — the more you worry, the heavier it gets.
Logline: Ten-year-old Milo's backpack has always been light as air — until it suddenly fills with fifty old worries, and he follows his big sister June up the lighthouse to learn she's been secretly carrying his worries every night since they moved to the island.
Twist (planted): June's backpack is "the size of a fridge" (10); Milo's is always light (12); the stones are old worries from the move (22-23); June's backpack shrinks the same week his grows (25). Payoff: half the stones in her pack have his name (30); he takes his back and one of hers — her math worry weighs nothing to him (39-40).
Characters:
- Milo, 10 (narrator): light skin, ginger curls, freckles, navy/white sailor-stripe tee, rust pants, yellow rain boots; teal backpack.
- June, 15 (big sister): brown skin, long black braids, round glasses, white headphones, sage cardigan, plum pants; coral backpack (fridge-sized).
- Captain Moss (ferry captain): pale, grey crew cut + full grey beard, wrinkles, navy uniform, white cap, orange life vest; dark-green backpack with an anchor.
- Baby Benny: red hoodie, tuft, one cracker in a tiny yellow backpack.
- Deckhands (no lines): goatee + red beanie; ponytail + blue bandana in a yellow jacket. Ferry kids: pink tee + puffs; green hoodie + spiky hair.
