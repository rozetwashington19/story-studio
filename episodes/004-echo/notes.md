# 004 — The Echo Woods
Mode: wild (world: forest night; everyday sets: kitchen, soccer field, street, kid room)
Premise: In this world, every echo comes back as a creature — the louder you yell, the bigger it gets.
Logline: Tobi, the loudest kid in Maple Hollow, yells "Nothing scares me!" in the forbidden Echo Woods and gets a tree-sized echo — so he beats it the only way an echo can be beaten: by yawning.
Characters:

- Wren, 10: pale skin, blonde pigtails + freckles, coral button shirt, navy jeans.
- Mr. Bram (teacher): tan skin, dark-brown crew cut + full beard, olive ranger shirt, khaki pants.
- Dad: brown skin, black buzz cut + mustache, blue shirt.
- Coach Dara: yellow skin, black bob, red tee.
- Echo creatures: Dinner Blob (orange one-eyed blob), Tobi's daytime echo "Fluff" (purple spotted blob), the giant "Nothing scares me" echo (brute, yellow eyes), the Calm echo (pale-blue tall blob), whistle bird (yellow), Goodnight wisp (tiny glowing).
