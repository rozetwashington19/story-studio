# 001 — Shadow
Premise: In this world, your shadow shows how you really feel.
Logline: Leo's shadow walks away. He finds it crying alone at the playground and realizes it carried all the sadness he hid after his best friend Max moved away.
Characters: Leo (tan, red hoodie, short dark hair), Mom (tan, purple top, long hair), Dad (light, blue shirt), Teacher (brown, black blazer, bun), Cousin (yellow, green tee, spiky orange hair), Max (light, teal hoodie, blond).
Sets: street, brick wall, classroom, dining/kitchen, kid's bedroom (day/night), hallway, stage, cafeteria, bus, airport, ocean, bakery, soccer field, playground (night), porch (night), backyard.
