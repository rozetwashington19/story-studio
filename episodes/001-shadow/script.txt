In this world,
your shadow shows how you really feel.
You see, your face can smile all day,
but your shadow can't lie.
My teacher says she loves Mondays.
Her shadow is lying face-down on the floor.
Dad says he loves Mom's meatloaf.
His shadow is spitting it into a napkin.
My cousin says his new haircut looks cool.
His shadow is wearing a hat.
So everyone in town is very careful where they stand.
Everyone except me.
I'm Leo. I'm twelve.
And three days ago,
my shadow got up and walked away.
Without me. [pause]
At first, it was kind of awesome.
"Leo, did you finish your homework?"
"Yep!" Nobody could check.
"Who ate the last cookie?"
"Not me!" Nobody could check.
I told my teacher my dog ate my homework.
I don't even have a dog.
But then it got weird.
Without a shadow, nobody could tell how I felt.
When I won the spelling bee, nobody clapped.
They didn't know if I was happy.
When I tripped in the hallway, nobody helped.
They didn't know if I was hurt.
When I told a joke, nobody laughed.
They didn't know if I was kidding.
And Mom kept staring at the empty floor next to my feet.
"Leo, are you okay?"
"I'm fine," I said.
She couldn't check. So she just looked sad.
So I went looking for it.
Under my bed. In the closet. Behind the fridge.
Nothing. [pause]
I found shadow handprints on the bakery window.
Shadow footprints across the soccer field.
And on Friday night, at the old playground,
I found it. [pause]
My shadow was sitting on the wall behind the swings.
Knees pulled up. Head down.
It was crying. [pause]
It was the saddest thing I've ever seen.
And that's when I remembered. [pause]

At the airport, Max cried.
I didn't. I just smiled and gave him a thumbs up.

At home, I told Mom I was totally fine.
I smiled so hard, for so long,

Every single night. [pause]
It didn't run away from me.
It just got tired of being sad alone. [pause]
So I sat down in the sand, right in front of it.

My shadow looked up. [pause]
Then it slid down off the wall,
across the sand,

She looked at my feet. Then she looked at my face.

She just hugged me. [pause]

Hers was crying too.
She'd known all along. [pause]

We sit out in the sun, together.

Even my cousin. His shadow finally took off the hat.
