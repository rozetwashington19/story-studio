# 020 — Noodle
Mode: wild (desert wishing-well town + a giant puppy; silly with a warm ending) — opener: cold-open (pick_mode). Hook = line 16 (giant Noodle behind the clock tower at sunset).
Angle: wrong-wish — Tess gets exactly what she asked for ("the biggest dog in the world") and it goes wrong; the fix is a second, better wish made by someone else.
Premise: Every coin dropped in the Penny Mesa well buys one wish — one per person, granted word for word.
Logline: New-in-town Tess spends her one wish on "the biggest dog in the world" and gets a forty-foot puppy the town wants gone by morning — until she remembers Noodle never made a wish of his own.
Twist / surprising beats: the well spits a second coin back out; the dog is the only one in town with a wish left; he "fetches" the coin down the well; the last shot is the tiny pup's giant shadow on the wall.
Gags: Mrs. Pell wished for a million followers (a million ducks); the baker wished for bread that never runs out (a baguette snaking out of his oven across the town for nine years — reused as the lunch bench).
Characters (cast in common.js):
- Tess, 15 (narrator): brown skin, black braids, mustard hoodie (coin in the pocket), teal jeans, teal headphones round her neck, white sneakers, teal wristband.
- Noodle: blocky golden puppy (bigPup), brown floppy ears and back patch, cream snout, red collar with a gold coin tag. Sizes: 0.3 (tiny) → 1.55 (car) → 2.4 (bus) → 7–9.5 (forty feet) → 0.42.
- Mrs. Pell, 70s: pale, silver bun, wrinkles, hot-pink blazer, sunglasses pushed up, white scarf, pink boots, phone selfie.
- The baker: tan, receding black hair + full beard, red bandana, blue-white striped shirt, floury apron.
- Mayor Hollis: light skin, grey slick hair + walrus mustache, white cowboy hat, tan blazer, turquoise bolo tie.
- Classmates (no lines): red tee + backwards cap (spiky), green sweater + glasses + ginger ponytail + freckles, purple jacket + afro + orange backpack. Townsfolk extras (overalls farmer, apron lady, polo).
Sets (all new, engine/sets20.js): pennyMesa (desert adobe town plaza — wishing well, bakery with oven hatch, clock tower, town hall, school, water tower, windmill, palo-verde tree, papel-picado flags, mesas + dunes; day / sunset / night), wellShaft (inside the well looking up), tessRoom (Tess's attic room with a round window, moving boxes, dog posters).
