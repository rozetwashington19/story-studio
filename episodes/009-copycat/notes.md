# 009 — Copycat Snow
Mode: wild:snow (silly, small heartfelt ending). Opener: in-this-world. Hook = line 27 (the giant stomping toward town).
Premise: In this world, snowmen come alive — and they copy whoever built them (what they do, and, it turns out, how they feel).
Twist: they don't just copy actions, they copy feelings (planted by Dad's napping snowman and Grandma's knitting one, paid off 29–32), so Nora calms down and lies down in the snow — the giant flops down too and makes a park-sized snow angel.
Logline: Nora hates losing, so she builds the biggest snowman ever for the Frost Fair — and when she throws a tantrum over second place, her giant copies it, until she figures out snowmen copy feelings and calms them both down with a snow angel.
Characters (2b — themed: snowy small town, winter clothes):
