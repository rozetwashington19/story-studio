# 005 — The Year Trader
Mode: heartfelt (dramatic) — owner request R1 (REQUESTS.md)
Premise: In this world, you can sell years of your life for money — everyone wears a glowing wrist clock that counts their years.
Logline: Kai sells forty of his years at the rainy Time Exchange to buy medicine for his always-tired mom — only to learn she isn't sick: she traded her own years to save him as a baby, and has been sweeping the shop's floors every night to buy them back one day at a time.
Mother's secret (planted clues): she always keeps her sleeve pulled down (lines 10–11); she slips out every night in the rain (13); Mr. Vesper says "You have your mother's eyes" (18). Payoff: lines 28–35.
Characters:
- Kai, 11 (narrator): yellow skin, dark-brown short hair (goes gray after the trade), teal hoodie, charcoal jeans, rosy cheeks. Clock 90 -> 50.
- Mom: brown skin, black low bun, burgundy long-sleeve shirt (sleeve hides her clock), slate pants. Clock 3 (red) -> 43, a little gray at the end.
- Mr. Vesper (shopkeeper): pale, white balding hair + walrus mustache, wrinkles, dark-green blazer.
- Mrs. Goldwin: light skin, platinum perm, pink blazer, white pants — 200 years young, cartwheels.
- Benny, 11: tan, ginger spiky hair + freckles, red tee — sells one year for a console, grows a mustache.
- Stranger (man with stubble, gray coat), three umbrella pedestrians, baby Kai (flashback).
