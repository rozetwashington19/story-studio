# 003 — The Kite String
Premise: In this world, every promise ties a glowing string between two pinkies. It stays until the promise is kept, and snaps if it's broken.
Logline: Rosa has had the same glowing promise string her whole life — when it starts to fade, she follows it up the hill to Grandpa, who has waited six years for her to keep a promise she forgot.
Characters: Rosa, 11 (light skin, ginger bun, teal tee, purple jeans), Grandpa Walt (light, white short hair, brown top hat, mustard cardigan-blazer), Mom (light, long black hair, plum shirt), Dad (light, short brown hair, green shirt), Benny, 7 (light, spiky ginger hair, orange tee, scaled 0.8), Mr. Ortiz (brown, bald, blue overalls-ish shirt), Priya (brown, long black hair, pink hoodie), little Rosa age 5 (scaled 0.7) in flashback.
